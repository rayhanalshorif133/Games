HOW TO PLAY

1. Move Pods & Terminals: Touch and drag any modifier box (like +1), the Dispenser Vault (start), or the Recipe Computer (end) to move them anywhere on the kitchen counter.


2. Wire the Path: Touch or drag from the Start Box output port to the first pod, then connect to the next pod, and finally connect into the Computer receiver port.


3. Cook by Number: Press START COOKING. The numbered ball will roll through each pod, transforming its number. Match the Computer's target to win!
